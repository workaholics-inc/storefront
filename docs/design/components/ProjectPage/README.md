# ProjectPage

A project and its record: title, one-liner, owners and stage, then the timeline of posts on a notebook margin line, with an aside for owners, visibility and the answer rule.

**Sections:** breadcrumbs · head (title-1, Public badge, one-liner in `text-lead`, owners · stage · since date, links) · timeline (newest first, labelled): update posts, work-in-progress posts (compact, with the unanswered chip and a link to the thread), and small entries such as "project created" · aside (`container-aside`, after the timeline below `lg`).

**Rules:** owners see «پست تازه» and «ویرایش پروژه» in the head; other members see none. A visitor sees only public posts. The timeline never paginates by "top"; it loads older entries on request.
