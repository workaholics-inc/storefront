# MemberProfile

A member's page: who they are, who invited them and whom they invited, their projects, and the feedback they gave with each item's current state.

**Sections:** head (initials, name in `text-title-1`, handle in LTR, bio, joined date, invited by) · projects (ProjectCards) · «بازخوردهایی که داده»: a list of items with project, date, the feedback text and its tag or chip · aside: invited by, invited, a line saying there are no followers or scores here.

**Rules:** no follower count, no follow button, no totals, no response rate, no streaks, no activity heatmap. Lists are in time order. The invitation tree links both ways.
